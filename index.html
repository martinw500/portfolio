Motor temperature over CAN

On UBC Solar's car I proposed measuring the motor's temperature with a PT1000 RTD and wrote the firmware from a breadboard prototype to the driver on the Motor Drive Interface board. Here is the path a reading takes.

  1. PT1000 RTDPlatinum sensor on the motor
  2. wire
  3. MAX31865Turns resistance into a reading
  4. SPI
  5. STM32 on the MDIMy driver and fault debounce
  6. CAN
  7. 0x502, once a secondSuccess, °C, fault flags
  8. telemetry
  9. GrafanaThe pit crew's dashboard

A missing chip doesn't cause an SPI error; it reads back as about −259 °C. So the driver reads its config register back before every conversion, and a fault has to last five reads before the flag latches, so one glitch never raises it.
